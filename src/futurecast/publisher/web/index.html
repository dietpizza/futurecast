The "Not a Finance-Bro" Cheat Sheet Plain-English explanations of every number on this page

Win probability

Win Long / short win
Chance that a trade entered now hits its take-profit before its stop-loss within the horizon. The models pick both levels for every candle from a few pairs scaled to recent volatility, taking the pair with the highest expected profit after fees; a timeout or touching both in one candle counts as a loss.
Needs Break-even
The win rate at which the trade makes zero after fees (0.10% round trip assumed), using how large past losses really were. Below it, the trade loses money on average.
Edge Proven edge
The probabilities scored better than simply quoting the usual win rate, on newer history the models never saw, with 90% confidence. Without it the page shows the usual (base) rate instead.
bps Basis points
1 bps = 0.01%. The order book spread is quoted in basis points.

Models & verification

Ens Ensemble
A blend of LightGBM, XGBoost and CatBoost. Blending cancels out each model's blind spots for a steadier forecast.
3/3 Model agreement
How many of the three models, on their own, point the same way as the signal. Disagreement means the direction is uncertain.
Test Tested on unseen data
Models train on older candles, are calibrated on the next stretch, and are scored on the newest stretch they never saw. "When it said go" replays every signal on that stretch, with the take-profit / stop pair the models would have picked, one trade at a time, after fees.
AUC Ranking quality
How well the model ranks winning trades above losing ones. 0.5 is a coin flip, 1.0 is perfect; crypto edges usually sit at 0.55–0.65.

Market activity

Book Order book imbalance
Buy versus sell limit orders waiting in the book. Heavy bids can cushion dips; heavy asks can cap rallies.
Risk Long / short risk
Trained models estimate how far price could move against each side in the worst 5% of cases. The 0–100 score ranks that against the coin's recent history; "Max" is the highest leverage that keeps liquidation beyond it.
CVD Cumulative volume delta
Aggressive buying versus price. Flat price with strong buying can precede a breakout.
Clock Volume clock
Trading burst intensity versus a quiet baseline. Low readings flag dormant, choppy markets.
