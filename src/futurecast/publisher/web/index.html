Structure & setups

BOS Break of structure
A candle closes beyond the latest swing high (or low) in the direction of the trend: the trend continues. A swing high is a candle whose high tops its neighbours on both sides, so it is only known a few candles later.
CHoCH Change of character
A close beyond the latest swing point against the trend, e.g. below the last higher low in an uptrend: the trend flips.
FVG Fair value gap
Three candles where the first candle's wick and the third candle's wick do not overlap: price skipped that range in one push. It stays open until a candle closes through its far side, and price often comes back into it.
A+ Setup
The higher (context) chart's trend points the trade's way, the lower (entry) chart's pullback reached into a context-chart gap, then an entry-chart CHoCH left a gap behind. Each card names its two charts. Orders: a limit at that gap's midpoint, a stop just past the pullback's extreme, a take-profit at 3 or 4 times the risk (R). Setups missing a context-chart check are grade B and never raise a signal.

Models & verification

Win Win chance
Chance a filled setup reaches its take-profit before its stop. LightGBM, XGBoost and CatBoost learn it from the coin's past setups; unless they beat the rules on setups they never saw, the rules' own win rate for the grade is shown instead.
Needs Break-even
The win rate at which a trade makes zero after fees. The limit entry and take-profit pay the maker fee; the stop is a market order that pays the taker fee and slips past its price, so a stopped trade costs more than a winner. Rates: CoinDCX USDT-M futures with 18% GST, shown in each card's Setups heading. Tight stops make fees a large share of the risk, so they need a higher win rate.
Test Tested on unseen history
Models train on older setups, are calibrated on the next stretch, and are scored on the newest stretch they never saw. That stretch is also replayed: every A+ setup (the rules), and the A+ setups the models liked, one at a time, after fees.
AUC Ranking quality
How well the win chances rank winning setups above losing ones. 0.5 is a coin flip, 1.0 is perfect.

Market activity

Book Order book imbalance
Buy versus sell limit orders waiting in the book. Heavy bids can cushion dips; heavy asks can cap rallies.
Risk Long / short risk
Trained models estimate how far price could move against each side in the worst 5% of cases, calibrated separately for calm, normal and stormy volatility so the 5% holds in rough stretches too. The 0–100 score ranks that against the coin's recent history; "Max" is the highest leverage that keeps liquidation beyond it.
CVD Cumulative volume delta
Aggressive buying versus price. Flat price with strong buying can precede a breakout.
Clock Volume clock
Trading burst intensity versus a quiet baseline. Low readings flag dormant, choppy markets.
